# Stage dumps of the conformance job (.github/workflows/build.yml, job "conformance", after the run;
# testing/stage_dump/ci_dumps.py; docs/warp-decoder.md, "Stage dumps").
#
#   <stream> <frames> [<temporal units>]
#
# <stream> is a name of testing/conformance/streams.txt; <frames> is a -dumpstages frame list (frames in
# decoding order from 0, hidden frames included: 0,3,10-12); <temporal units> stops the decode after that
# many temporal units of the stream (warp_decoder -limit; default: the whole stream).
#
# The job's artifact rav1g-stage-dumps-<run id> has, per stream, what warp_decoder wrote on WARP:
#   aom and svt streams: the dumps themselves (<stream>_f<n>.stages). An svt stream is the encoder's test
#     pattern, encoded in the job, and comes along in the artifact (streams/), so that another decoder can be
#     given the very same file;
#   cut streams: digests only (<stream>_f<n>.digest: a hash per row and per 8x8 block). They are published
#     media, and neither the streams nor their decoded frames leave the job.
# A dump of a frame is ten to twenty times the size of the frame: keep the lists short.
# A stream that starts a sequence which takes a new decoder (RECONFIGURE) has its frames numbered from 0
# again behind it, in files <stream>.seq<n>_f<frame>: the frame list applies to every sequence.
